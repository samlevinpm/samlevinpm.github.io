About Me

Mission-driven PM. Collaborative builder. Lifelong learner.

I'm a Senior Product Manager with 7+ years of experience — 5+ of them building fintech SaaS for financial institutions. A background in public health trained me to stay outcome- and people-focused, and to track what matters. That same discipline shows up across everything I do: interviewing clients, shaping roadmaps, collaborating with engineering and design, and validating hypotheses with data. No two days look the same.

Previously at MotionMobs, a custom software development firm, I led 20+ products from discovery to launch — implementing Agile practices, scaling team capacity, and shipping a COVID contact-tracing app in partnership with Apple, Google, and Alabama public health authorities.

Outside of work, I enjoy trail running, SCUBA diving, traveling, and reading.
